Cancellation & Refund Policy

How cancellations, refunds and product guarantees work at BlueSky Agency.

Last updated: 24 August 2026
The short version: everything we sell is digital and is delivered to you immediately, so we do not offer refunds. You can cancel a subscription at any time and keep using it until the month you have already paid for runs out. Where a product carries a specific guarantee, we replace it rather than refund it.

1. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a monthly plan whenever you like — there is no lock-in period and no cancellation fee. To cancel, message us on WhatsApp or email info@blueskyage.in from the address on your account.

When you cancel:

  • The current month is not refunded. You have already paid for it, so you keep full access for the rest of that month.
  • Billing stops from the next month onward. Nothing further is charged.
  • At the end of the paid month the ad account is disabled.

In other words: cancel on day 3 of the month and you still keep the account until day 30. You simply are not billed again.

2. Money left in your ad account

If your ad account still holds unspent ad funds when it is disabled, that balance is not lost. We transfer it to your next ad account with us, so you can carry on spending it.

Please note that ad funds themselves are not refundable to your bank account or card. Once money has been loaded into an ad account it can only be used for advertising — either on that account or on a replacement account we provide. This is how the upstream ad platform works, and we cannot reverse it.

3. Digital products are final sale

Pages, profiles, Instagram accounts and every other one-time product we sell are digital goods. The moment we hand over the credentials, the product has been delivered and cannot be taken back or resold — so these purchases are non-refundable.

4. When a product carries a guarantee

Some products are sold with a specific guarantee, for example a 24-hour login guarantee. If the product fails within that window, we honour the guarantee by replacing it with an equivalent product at no extra cost. Replacement is the remedy — a cash refund is not offered.

To claim a guarantee, contact us within the stated window with your order details. Claims raised after the guarantee period has passed cannot be accepted.

5. What is not covered

  • Accounts restricted or banned because of how they were used, including policy violations on the ad platform.
  • Credentials changed, shared or lost after handover.
  • A change of mind after a digital product has been delivered.
  • Advertising results — we provide the account and support, not a guarantee of campaign performance.

6. How to reach us

For any cancellation or guarantee claim, contact us on WhatsApp at +91 95071 96648 or email info@blueskyage.in. Include your invoice number so we can find your order quickly. We aim to respond within one business day.
