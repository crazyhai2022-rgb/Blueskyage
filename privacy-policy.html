Privacy Policy

How BlueSky Agency collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This policy explains what BlueSky Agency collects when you use blueskyage.in, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number and password when you create an account; your business name, Business Portfolio (BM) ID, advertising niche and expected ad spend when you place an order; and anything you write to us in a message or contact form.

Information collected automatically: basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, which help us keep the site working and secure.

Payment information: card and banking details are entered directly with our payment provider, Razorpay. They are never sent to or stored on our servers. We only receive a payment reference and whether the payment succeeded.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and dashboard.
  • To set up, deliver and support the ad accounts and products you buy.
  • To issue invoices and keep the financial records the law requires.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of the service.

3. Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google so that we can create your account. We never receive your Google password, and we do not post anything to your Google account.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services that make our business run:

  • Razorpay — to process payments.
  • Supabase — to store account and order records.
  • Our hosting provider — to serve the website.
  • Government authorities — where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is active and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete your account at any time; we will remove what we are not legally obliged to retain.

6. Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form. Traffic to the site is encrypted over HTTPS, and access to order data is restricted to people who need it to do their job. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these safeguards seriously.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct anything wrong, or delete your account. Email info@blueskyage.in from your registered address and we will act on it.

8. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. It is required for the account area to work and disappears when your session ends. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be communicated to account holders.
